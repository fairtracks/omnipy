## Command-line Cryptic — Visual Style Summary

**Command-line Cryptic** is a **middle-aged to older male hacker-avatar villain**, gaunt and worn down, with the look of someone who has spent decades living inside terminals, cables, and outdated systems. He should feel like a **legendary but socially isolated old-school programmer**, now fully transformed into the ruler of a bleak digital labyrinth.

### Core Character Look

* **Age:** late middle-aged to elderly
* **Build:** thin, slightly hunched, wiry, sleep-deprived
* **Face:** lined, tired, intelligent, severe; unshaven or with rough stubble
* **Hair:** thinning, messy, receding, unkempt
* **Eyes:** intense, suspicious, deeply focused; often magnified by glasses
* **Glasses:** large, practical, slightly outdated programmer glasses
* **Expression:** smug, disdainful, superior, socially awkward, emotionally detached

### Clothing / Costume

* Wears **shabby layered clothing** rather than a theatrical costume
* Old hoodie, worn jacket, faded t-shirt, utility pockets, patched trousers
* Clothing should look **functional, neglected, and lived-in**
* The shirt or jacket may include programmer slogans like:

  * “vim is all you need”
  * “real programmers don’t use IDEs”
  * “no IDE zone”
* Accessories can include:

  * pens in pocket
  * clipped badges or tags
  * cable loops
  * keycards
  * mugs
  * old hardware bits
  * belt pouches or dangling tools

### Aesthetic Identity

He is **not a noble count in a cape**. He is the **avatar of terminal purity**:

* a **command-line absolutist**
* a **monochrome systems-code zealot**
* a man who worships **Perl, Fortran, Assembly, C, PHP+CSS, and Vim**
* hostile toward modern abstractions, IDEs, frameworks, and “friendly” interfaces

His whole design should communicate:

* **old-school computing purity**
* **grimy technical mastery**
* **monochrome elitism**
* **labyrinthine logic**
* **hostile minimalism**

### Color Palette

* Predominantly **monochrome** or near-monochrome
* Charcoal black, gray, off-white, muted brown, dirty beige
* Very restrained color accents, if any:

  * faint terminal green
  * muted amber
  * dull phosphor glow
* Overall mood should feel **cold, gray, terminal-lit, and exhausted**

### Environment

He belongs inside a **dense code labyrinth**:

* walls of old monitors
* stacked CRTs or terminal screens
* cables everywhere
* text panels, pointer addresses, hex dumps
* keyboard-heavy workstation
* old mugs, papers, manuals, sticky notes
* cluttered but controlled technical chaos

The setting should feel like:

* a **maze of pointers, buffers, and memory addresses**
* a **grim kingdom of terminals**
* a **lair built from code, screens, and obsolete mastery**

### Graphic / Narrative Style

The approved image presents him like a **comic-book villain introduction page**:

* bold title at the top
* dramatic subtitle
* comic narration boxes
* speech balloon with catchphrase
* embedded code snippets and terminal screens
* gritty poster-like composition
* strong central character pose
* visual storytelling through environment and text fragments

### Overall Mood

He should feel like:

* **dangerous through obscurity rather than violence**
* **pathetic and powerful at the same time**
* **a bitter wizard of legacy systems**
* **the human embodiment of unreadable output**
* **a villain who believes he is right**

## Short Prompt-Ready Version

**Command-line Cryptic** is a gaunt, middle-aged-to-elderly hacker villain avatar: shabby, sleep-deprived, socially awkward, and intensely intelligent. He has thinning messy hair, stubble, large old-fashioned glasses, and a smug, superior expression. He wears worn programmer clothes—hoodie, faded t-shirt, patched jacket, practical trousers—with slogans like “vim is all you need” or “real programmers don’t use IDEs.” His aesthetic is grimy, monochrome, and old-school: Perl, Fortran, Assembly, C, hand-written PHP+CSS, and terminal purity. He lives inside a bleak labyrinth of CRTs, code panes, cables, hex dumps, memory pointers, and command-line screens. The color palette is mostly black, gray, dirty beige, and faint terminal green. The style should feel like a gritty comic-book villain introduction: dramatic title, narration boxes, speech balloons, code fragments, and a dense, hostile, terminal-lit atmosphere.
