**The Null Ninja — Visual Style Summary**

The Null Ninja is a female, hooded data-assassin whose body feels only partly real. She should be drawn as an elegant, spectral ninja figure: athletic, practical, quiet, and dangerous, with a feminine silhouette that remains subtle rather than glamorous. Her presence is defined by absence. She appears as if she is already dissolving out of the scene, leaving behind smoke, missing pixels, corrupted timestamps, and traces of `NaN`.

Her face is deeply shrouded by a hood, scarf, veil, and shadow. Do not give her a clear gaze or memorable beautiful eyes. The eyes should be hidden or almost entirely lost in darkness. Only faint glimpses of skin should show around the nose, cheeks, mouth, or lower face. Her expression should be a vague, restrained, unsettling half-smile, not cute or inviting. The smile should feel like she knows something has already vanished.

Her clothing is layered and practical: dark ninja wraps, hooded cloak, scarf, loose trailing fabric, fitted tactical cloth, fingerless wrappings, and subtle armor-like panels. Use mostly black, charcoal, gray, and ink-wash textures, with restrained violet accents. Repeating visual marks include the null/empty-set symbol, small violet glyphs, `NaN`, broken timestamps, and disappearing data fragments.

Her hands are important. They should show visible skin, five clear fingers, practical fingerless wraps or gloves, and short-to-medium feminine nails that are neat but functional. Her hands have quiet erasure magic: wherever her fingertips touch, evidence unthreads into purple-black smoke, dissolving pixels, null-symbol particles, fading ink, and missing pieces of reality. The effect should be elegant and subtle, not explosive.

The preferred illustration style is a refined comic-book dossier sheet: cream paper background, black pen-and-ink drawing, cross-hatching, brush shadows, sparse violet highlights, handwritten scientific annotations, tiny tables, p-value sketches, corrupted timestamp notes, null charms, and head or hand studies. The composition should feel like a character design file rather than a movie poster.

Overall mood: mysterious, restrained, spectral, intelligent, surgical, and quietly threatening. She does not attack loudly. She erases meaning, leaves logs clean, and is remembered only by what disappeared.
