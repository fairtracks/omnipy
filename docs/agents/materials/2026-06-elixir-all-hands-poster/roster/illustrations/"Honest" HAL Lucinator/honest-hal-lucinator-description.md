**“Honest” HAL Lucinator — Final Visual Style Guide**

A stop-motion / handmade fabric-and-clay advertisement style, like a highly polished children’s TV commercial crossed with a shady web popup. The entire image should look physically constructed from textured felt, clay, foam, stitched fabric, raised rubber letters, and chunky handmade signage. Surfaces are tactile, slightly bumpy, soft-edged, and three-dimensional, with visible craft texture rather than smooth digital gradients.

HAL is a grinning, overconfident used-data salesman: broad toothy smile, raised eyebrows, slick black pompadour hair, bright blue eyes, purple suit jacket, green circuit-pattern vest, tie, and a small “HAL” name badge. He points directly at the viewer in an exaggerated sales-pitch pose. His expression should feel charming, ridiculous, suspiciously confident, and slightly untrustworthy, but still playful and poster-friendly.

The color palette is bold but warm: creamy off-white background, deep purple framing, green “AI credits” text, yellow/gold banners and arrows, teal-blue ontology boxes, and strong black text for readability. The lighting should be bright, studio-lit, and cheerful, with soft shadows that make every object feel like a real miniature prop.

Typography should be large, chunky, handmade, and very readable at poster scale. Major text appears as raised felt or foam letters. Important phrases use high-contrast colors and simple layouts. Avoid small dense UI clutter for the poster ad; reserve that for the full web-page companion image.

The ad composition should feel like an over-the-top fake promotional insert: huge headline “FREE AI CREDITS!”, ridiculous sales banner such as “NO CARD! NO COST! NO PROBLEM!™ (FOR US)”, a speech bubble from HAL, an ontology diagram, a QR placeholder, and a bottom brand strip. The QR placeholder should be large, plain white, rectangular, and clearly labeled “PLACE WORKING QR HERE.”

The ontology gag is central. Include a clean vertical “Ontology (totally legit)” hierarchy panel using stacked boxes connected by arrows, showing:

Thing → Vehicle → Aircraft → Boeing 747 → Homo sapiens

This should be readable and visually prominent, functioning as the scientific joke: HAL confidently treats a biological species as a subclass of an aircraft. The ontology panel should look like it is built from layered felt or clay labels.

Use the Lucinator.ai branding, but avoid any face/logo that could resemble blackface. The logo should instead be a harmless cute robot icon or abstract AI/ontology symbol, preferably with a light faceplate, purple casing, simple friendly eyes, and headphone/antenna details.

The speech bubble tone should be concise, absurdly confident, and FAIR-data/bioinformatics flavored. Final small-ad wording:

“100% resolvable identifiers!*
Sign up today!”

With nearby fine print:

“* somewhere, probably”

Overall mood: playful, tactile, satirical, bioinformatics-aware, highly readable, and suitable as a smaller poster ad that links via QR code to a more detailed full-page fake web ad.
