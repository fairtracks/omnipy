Lord Franken-Format — The Toxic Feeder — is a dark comic-book supervillain in a gothic, Batman-like graphic-novel style. He is not a flesh-and-body-parts Frankenstein monster; he is a towering humanoid construct made from physical sources of messy data and obsolete documentation. His body is assembled from paper printouts, tabular reports, binders, user manuals, technical reference books, floppy disks, punched cards, USB cables, keyboard keys, plugs, ports, folders, sticky notes, labels, and battered office hardware. These materials are not decorations; they form his anatomy. His chest is a stack of incompatible spreadsheets and printouts. His shoulders are built from manuals and binders. His arms are wrapped in USB cables and old connectors. His fingers resemble keyboard keys and data-entry buttons. His face is a grim mask of layered paper, labels, metal clips, and cracked plastic data-storage fragments.

His silhouette should be massive, angular, and imposing, like a tragic industrial gargoyle made of bad metadata. He wears a ragged cape or coat formed from overlapping paper reports, manuals, punched-card strips, and broken schema documentation. Visible integrated details may include labels such as CSV, TSV, YAML, XML, JSON, API, XLSX, “USER GUIDE,” “TECHNICAL REFERENCE,” “DATABASE DESIGN,” “deprecated but still here,” “DO NOT DELETE,” “backup??,” and mismatched header names. His construction should feel overloaded, brittle, and physically heavy, as if decades of incompatible formats have accumulated into a single cursed body.

His expression is villainous first: a cold green glare, severe brow, and malicious, commanding sneer. However, underneath the evil there is a faint sadness or exhaustion, as if he knows he is a grotesque consequence of neglected data practices. He is not goofy or purely sympathetic; he is dangerous, bitter, and toxic, with just enough melancholy to make him feel like a fallen artifact of scientific chaos.

The visual style is dark, inked, and theatrical: strong black outlines, sharp angular shadows, gothic-industrial architecture, deep purple-blue backgrounds, toxic acid-green highlights, gritty comic texture, and dramatic low-angle composition. The mood should resemble a noir superhero comic poster: sinister, polished, high-contrast, and over-the-top. He is usually shown in a ruined data-cathedral or industrial pipeline chamber, surrounded by pipes, cables, warning screens, corrupted printouts, and glowing green sludge.

His signature action is emptying a heavy barrel labeled “DATA SLUDGE” into a funnel or pipeline machine. The sludge glows toxic green and carries fragments of broken formats, schema errors, tiny tables, bits of code, and floating paper scraps. Nearby machinery should show pipeline failure: “SCHEMA NOT FOUND,” “TYPE MISMATCH,” “NULL APOCALYPSE,” or similar red terminal warnings. Foreground pipes may be labeled “INGEST,” “VALIDATE,” and “LOAD.”

When used in a poster layout, include the title “Lord Franken-Format,” the subtitle “The Toxic Feeder,” and the catchphrase: “Why choose one standard when you can have pieces of ALL OF THEM?!” The typography should be distressed gothic-comic lettering with slime drips, torn-paper panels, and toxic green accents.
