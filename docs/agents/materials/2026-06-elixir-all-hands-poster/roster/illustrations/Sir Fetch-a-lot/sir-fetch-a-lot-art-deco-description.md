Here is the definitive visual style summary for **Sir Fetch-a-Lot**, perfectly tailored to serve as a style guide or prompt blueprint for future illustrations in this exact universe:

## Style Guide: Classic Art Deco Cybernetics

### 1. Core Aesthetic & Influence

* **Art Deco Fusion:** A seamless blend of 1930s high-end Art Deco elegance and retro-futuristic sci-fi. The design avoids messy, gritty cyberpunk elements in favor of clean symmetry, sweeping streamlined curves, and immaculate geometric detailing.
* **Presentation:** Characters are depicted in a heroic, proud stance, often positioned atop a tiered geometric pedestal that features a built-in, framed metal name-tag banner.

### 2. Color Palette

* **Primary Metals:** Polished warm gold, brushed brass, and rich bronze form the primary structure of the character.
* **Accent Metals:** Sleek chrome and polished silver are used for high-contrast areas like wings, joints, and mechanical feathers.
* **Background Colors:** Deep teal, muted emerald green, and dark slate tones provide a sophisticated, high-contrast backdrop that makes the golden metals pop.
* **Light Emissives:** Terminal green for digital lenses/screens and a bright, vibrant prismatic rainbow spectrum emanating from power cores.

### 3. Character Design & Anatomy (Mechanical Canine)

* **True Canine Form:** The character must strictly maintain actual canine proportions (specifically a friendly, noble Labrador/Golden Retriever build) rather than abstract or monstrous anatomy.
* **Plating & Joints:** Smooth, overlapping metallic plates mimic the musculature of a dog. Joints are clean, visible, and mechanical without exposed oily gears or loose wiring.
* **Intricate Engravings:** Subtle, elegant filigree or geometric chevrons are etched directly onto the torso plating to give it a handcrafted, premium look.

### 4. Key Signature Features

* **Mechanical Wings:** Large, majestic, swept-back mechanical wings composed of layered, silver-chrome metallic feathers. The design is sleek and aerodynamic, reminiscent of classic hood ornaments.
* **The Prismatic Chest Core:** A large, multi-faceted diamond crystal is securely slotted directly into the center of the chest plate, casting a brilliant rainbow glow outward.
* **Digital Code Visor:** Instead of eyes or laser beams, the character wears chunky, retro-futuristic aviator goggles. The lenses are flat green computer screens displaying glowing, matrix-style vertical terminal readouts and HTTP status codes.

### 5. Background & Framing

* **Symmetrical Borders:** The image is enclosed by a highly stylized, symmetrical Art Deco border frame featuring classic motifs: sunburst rays, nested chevrons, and sweeping fans.
* **Geometric Accents:** Sharp, multi-pointed geometric starbursts and diamond sparkles are scattered symmetrically in the background to emphasize a clean, celestial glamour.

### 6. Rendering & Art Style

* **Line Art:** Crisp, clean, defined black ink outlines provide structure, akin to high-end graphic novels or animation pitch art.
* **Shading & Highlights:** Smooth, soft-gradient metallic shading paired with sharp, high-intensity white highlights gives every surface a tangible, polished, and reflective metallic texture.
